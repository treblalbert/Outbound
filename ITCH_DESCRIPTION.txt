OUTBOUND
========

A top-down open-world extraction shooter. Leave the bunker, loot a world
that is rebuilt fresh for every new day, and get back to the hatch before
22:00. Miss the deadline and the night arrives with more of them than you
can ever kill.

----------------------------------------
THE LOOP
----------------------------------------

Sleep in the bunker to save and heal. Head outside into a world generated
from scratch for that day: terrain, towns, roads, camps, cars, loot and
raiders -- nothing is placed by hand. Search every cabinet, locker, crate
and corpse, then carry it all home and sell it at the trader. Spend the
money on guns, armor, backpacks and meds, or on permanent upgrades at the
workbench.

The further you get from the hatch, the better the loot -- and the more
people who would rather you did not take it. Every day outside is deadlier
than the last: tougher enemies, in greater numbers, hitting harder.

Duck back inside and out again on the same day and the world remembers
what you did -- where you explored, who you killed, which containers you
already emptied. Sleep, and it rolls a whole new one.

----------------------------------------
THE DEADLINE
----------------------------------------

Your clock runs about 16 in-game hours. The hatch seals at 22:00. If you
are still outside when night falls, the horde spawns endlessly and hunts
you down -- no extraction, no mercy. Die out there and you lose everything
you were carrying.

----------------------------------------
FEATURES
----------------------------------------

* A brand-new open world every day, generated procedurally.
* 12 weapons -- pistols, SMG, revolver, shotgun, carbine, rifle, sniper
  and a rocket launcher -- plus grenades, armor and backpacks.
* Fully destructible terrain: bullets chew through walls and trees,
  explosions level everything nearby.
* Roofed buildings you cannot see into until you step through the door.
  Blow the walls away and the roof caves in for good.
* Raider camps, towns, compounds and roadblocks to clear or avoid.
* A bunker home base: bed, stash, trader and a workbench with 8 upgrade
  lines across 3-8 levels each.
* Daily mission board with rewards for loot runs, kill counts, weapon
  recovery and supply retrieval.
* Full lighting and day/night cycle -- flashlights, street lights, muzzle
  flash and a night vibe that closes in around you.
* Three save slots, keyboard + mouse, and full Xbox controller support.
* Interface in English and Spanish, switchable any time.

----------------------------------------
CONTROLS
----------------------------------------

WASD      Move                  E     Interact / search
Shift     Sprint (stamina)      Tab   Inventory
Mouse     Aim                   M     Map
LMB       Shoot                 G     Throw grenade
R         Reload                H     Quick heal
1/2/Q     Switch weapon         Esc   Pause / close
Wheel     Cycle weapons         F11   Fullscreen

Gamepads supported: left stick moves, click sprints, right stick aims and
drives the UI cursor, RT shoots, A interacts, X reloads, Y heals.

----------------------------------------
ABOUT
----------------------------------------

Built from scratch in C++ and OpenGL, with no engine. Runs on Windows
(64-bit) and in the browser via WebAssembly -- the web build plays right
here on itch.io.

Artwork and sound come from The Lazy Stone (Post-apocalypse Pixel Art),
Darkworld Audio (Survival Effects) and Rusted Studio (Anemoia).

By Albert Freeman.
